What is MetaLab?

MetaLab is a collection of community-augmented meta-analyses of language acquisition and cognitive development. Anyone can use the data to explore phenomena in early development, plan a study with meta-analytically grounded power analyses, or contribute new effect sizes to a growing, curated database.

Explore

Interactive visualizations of every dataset: effect sizes across age, funnel plots, forest plots, and moderator analyses.

Plan

Meta-analytic power analysis: how many participants do you need, given everything the literature already knows about your effect?
